Our Story

Where Beauty Meets Modern Luxury.

S&U Beauty Journey

Thoughtful beauty, beautifully delivered.

S&U Professional Luxury Unisex Salon is a beauty destination in Sanjauli, Shimla, focused on professional hair, makeup, skincare, and personal-care experiences in a refined salon environment. Every detail is designed around comfort, confidence, and a polished finish.

From beauty consultations to styling, grooming and skin-focused rituals, the approach is personal, modern and elevated—without losing the warmth of a true boutique salon experience.
